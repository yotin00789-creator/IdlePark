# App shell and all admin surfaces (Operate)

Mode: Operate. Extend the established TailAdmin world already in code; the user's brief pins the aesthetic (TailAdmin minimal, small frameless line icons, IBM Plex Sans Thai). No concept tournament.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A household ledger, not a parking-lot console. One quiet working surface where the number you typed and every total that depends on it change together. Refuses the hero-metric template, icon-plus-heading card grids as page structure, and status/occupancy language: the lot is rented monthly, nothing here says "parked".

OWN-WORLD: TailAdmin tokens (brand #465FFF, success/warning/error scales, gray ramp, white cards, 1px borders, rounded-2xl cards, shadow-theme-xs). IBM Plex Sans Thai 400/500/600, tabular-nums right-aligned for money, 14px base on desktop, 16px on mobile inputs. Icons: 16-18px, stroke currentColor, no container, no tinted square. Signature moves: the plate chip for the car number, and the 12-month status strip per car. Light and dark follow TailAdmin.

STORY: Open on the phone at the kitchen table or on the laptop: see who has not paid this month, tap, record, and watch the totals move. Admin sees why a number is what it is by opening its Excel formula.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar left (desktop) or slide-over (mobile), header with bell and profile. Dashboard: left-aligned page title, one row of four metrics that differ in size by importance, then monthly chart beside the unpaid list.

FORM: Standard TailAdmin shell, data table with pinned columns on desktop, per-car cards with bottom sheet on mobile.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
